What is Hack2Hire?

Hack2Hire is not just a hackathon — it's a hiring challenge. Build a real-world solution, impress the industry, and walk away with a Paid Internship.

#BuildToGetHired #IndustryChallenge #PaidInternship

What Are We Doing?

Phase 01
🛠

Ideate

Kickstart your journey with a 1-week online build phase. From the theme drop, brainstorm and develop your most innovative tech solution with your Team of 2 members (mandatory).

Phase 02
⚡

Showcase

The elite Top 10 finalists will step onto the big stage! Present your innovation live before a prestigious panel of industry veterans and expert judges for the ultimate hackathon glory.

Phase 03
🏆

Succeed

Claim your victory and walk away with the coveted Paid Internships, a significant prize pool, and merit certificates. This is your launchpad for a blockbuster career in the tech industry.

🎓
Open to: 2nd & 3rd Year Students of CSE Department (CSE A, CSE B, DS, IoT) — T John Institute of Technology

Hack2Hire 1.0 is the flagship hackathon organized by T John Institute of Technology (TJIT). This event bridges the gap between academic talent and industry demands. Whether you're a seasoned developer or a passionate beginner, this is your stage to shine.

Rules & Guidelines

  • ✅ Teams of exactly 2 members only — no solo registrations
  • ✅ No pre-built or existing projects allowed
  • ✅ On Day 1, create a GitHub repo and push your README with the problem statement
  • ✅ Fill the Google Form shared on Day 1 with your repo link, project theme, and details
  • ✅ All payments must be completed before the build phase begins (via HelloCB App)
  • ✅ Submit before April 22 deadline — no extensions
  • ✅ GitHub commit history will be evaluated — commit regularly!
  • ✅ README must be descriptive and clearly explain your project
  • ✅ You may use any tools, frameworks, or AI assistants
  • ✅ Bonus points available for extra evaluation criteria
